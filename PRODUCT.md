## Platform

web

## Stack

The website lives as a claude.ai Design canvas (`.dc.html` artboard, `Main.dc.html`) at https://claude.ai/artifact/F9EXD1Dv7PaBuJPs618BWK. Markup is `<x-dc>` templates with a `DCLogic` class; no build step.

## Users

Owners of small businesses of any kind (shops, clinics, studios, restaurants, trades) who need a website that brings in customers and do not want to manage the technology themselves.

## Product Purpose

Sterling Growth Systems designs custom, mobile-first websites and trains an AI chat assistant on each client's business so visitors get instant answers and leads land in the owner's inbox. Success is a booked free strategy call.

## Positioning

A custom website and an AI assistant trained on the client's own business, delivered together, live in as little as 7 days, with a plain-English monthly growth report included.

## Operating Context

Free strategy call (about [20] minutes, times shown in [Central Time]) → design and setup (days 2–6, private preview link, revisions) → launch (day 7) → ongoing care with monthly reports on visitors, conversions, time on site, top assistant questions and next steps.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pricing (confirmed real): website only $1,500 standard / $2,000 advanced; website + AI chatbot $2,000 standard / $2,500 advanced plus a monthly retainer of $250 / $400 / $500 by company size (small / medium / large); monthly plan with no upfront cost $500 / $650 / $800 by size. The monthly plan always includes the AI chatbot and has no build level.
- The page must keep: the live assistant preview, the before/after comparison slider (Landscaping, HVAC samples), the pricing calculator, the FAQ, the booking calendar with time slots and the validated contact form.
