Common questions

What does the AI assistant actually do?

It sits in the corner of your website and answers visitors' questions about your services, prices, hours and anything else you tell it. When someone's interested, it takes their details or points them to your booking calendar.

What happens if it doesn't know the answer?

It won't make things up. It lets the visitor know someone will get back to them, grabs their contact details and sends the question your way.

How quickly can my site go live?

In as little as 7 days. Bigger projects can take a little longer, and you'll get a clear timeline before we start.

Can I make changes myself after launch?

Yes. We'll show you how to update text, photos and the info your assistant uses. If you'd rather not, our care plan has you covered.

What's in the monthly report?

Visitors and clicks, conversions like calls, form fills and bookings, how long people stay on your site, your most popular pages and the top questions your AI assistant was asked. Every report ends with clear recommendations for what we'll improve next.

How much will it cost?

Websites start at $1,500, and website plus AI chatbot packages start at $2,000 with a small monthly retainer. Prefer no upfront cost? Our monthly plan starts at $500 a month. The calculator above shows your exact price.
